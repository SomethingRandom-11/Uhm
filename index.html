```

My Love,

Sometimes I sit quietly and think about you, and it amazes me how someone who lives 1,400 kilometers away can feel closer to my heart than anyone standing right beside me.

You make ordinary days feel a little brighter and ordinary moments feel worth remembering.

Every day, I miss you in a hundred different ways. I miss your messages when you're busy, I miss hearing your voice when we haven't talked for a while, and I miss all the moments we haven't had the chance to share yet. But even with all this distance between us, my love for you only grows stronger.

If there is one thing I know for certain, it's that no amount of kilometers can measure what you mean to me. If I had the chance to see you for just ten minutes, I would travel across countries, cross oceans, and spend countless hours on the road without thinking twice. Ten minutes with you would be worth more than days spent anywhere else, because every second with you feels precious.

You are genuinely one of the most beautiful people I have ever known. Not just because of your smile, which could brighten even my darkest day, or your eyes, which I could get lost in forever. Your beauty comes from the way you care, the way you laugh, the way you make me feel understood, and the way your presence can turn an ordinary day into something unforgettable..

There are moments when I wish I could simply reach through the distance and hold your hand. I wish I could be there to see your smile in person, to walk beside you, to watch the sunset with you, or just sit quietly together doing nothing at all. Those simple moments are the ones I dream about most.

You know, out of all the memories we've created together, one shines brighter than the rest. The day you wrote me that love letter. I still remember how I felt reading every word. No one had ever written a love letter to me before. It was the first love letter I had ever received in my entire life, and it came from the person I love the most. That letter wasn't just words on a page—it was a piece of your heart given to me. I will cherish it forever, no matter how much time passes. Whenever life feels difficult, that memory reminds me how lucky I am to have you.

Thank you for loving me despite the distance. Thank you for being patient through the waiting,and all the moments when we wish we could be together. Every day we spend apart is simply one day closer to the moment we finally meet.

Yours ❤️,

Rohan
```
